# 001 · Brand name: Insi House

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** accepted
- **Context:** Needed a short, easy name that works in Arabic, Urdu/Hindi and English, feels premium and gift-like, and connects to the family.
- **Options considered:** Tohfa, Hadiya, Azam, Noor, Zari, House of Insiya, Insi Mart, Insi Bazaar, Insi & Co.
- **Decision:** **Insi House**, Arabic **بيت إنسي**, handle/domain `insihouse`.
  - "Insi" comes from the owner's niece **Insiya** (إنسية: warm, affectionate, close companion; root *uns* = closeness).
  - "House" gives a family-heritage, luxury-boutique feel.
  - "Mart" was rejected because it sounds like a grocery or discount store.
- **Consequences:**
  - Check domain and social handle availability, and trademark in India and the launch GCC countries.
  - Note: in Arabic, إنسي also means "human / of people". This is neutral and warm, so it is acceptable.
  - Collection names can carry the "gift / luxury" meaning: Nafees, Tohfa, Nazakat.
