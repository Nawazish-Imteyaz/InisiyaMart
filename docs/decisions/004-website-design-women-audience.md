# 004 · Website design tuned for women & students

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** proposed (owner to review the 9 page designs)
- **Context:** Owner says most buyers will be girls, women and students, so the site must feel attractive to them while staying professional.
- **Decision:**
  - Palette shifts from dark kohl to **Berry Plum + Blush + Dusty Rose + Ivory**, with gold as a small accent (`docs/brand/colors-fonts.md`).
  - Signature motif: **arch-shaped (mehrab) photo frames** throughout, echoing the logo.
  - Rounded pill buttons, soft cards, generous white space, serif headings (Cormorant Garamond) and clean body text (Manrope).
  - Women-focused features: "Gifts for her" category, name engraving, message cards in EN / AR / Urdu, gift-wrap choice, surprise delivery, date reminders, WhatsApp tracking.
  - 9 page designs in `docs/design/` (see its README).
- **Consequences:** The Next.js build uses these tokens and layouts. Arabic copy must get native Gulf review before launch.
