# 04 · Azamgarh Crafts & Product Knowledge

> General background on Azamgarh crafts. **The actual product list comes from the owner** (`08-brand-intake.md`).
> Only claim GI tags, materials or "handmade" for a product when the owner has confirmed it for that item.

## Azamgarh at a glance
- District in eastern **Uttar Pradesh, India**, with a rich Urdu literary and cultural heritage
  (associated with poets and scholars such as Kaifi Azmi and Shibli Nomani).
- Many people from Azamgarh live in other Indian cities: a natural, nostalgic audience.

## Storytelling rules
- Every product page has a "Story of this craft" block: origin, technique, time to make, and maker.
- Use real photos of the family and workshop if the family agrees. Authenticity sells.
- Avoid poverty framing. Present makers as skilled artists.
