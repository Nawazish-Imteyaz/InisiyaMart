# 06 · India Shipping, Tax & Legal

> ⚠️ Not legal or tax advice. **Verify each item with a Chartered Accountant before launch.**

## Shipping
| Option | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Shiprocket** (aggregator) | Recommended start | One dashboard for Delhivery, Blue Dart, Xpressbees, Ekart, DTDC; COD remittance; labels; tracking pages; WhatsApp updates |
| Delhivery / Blue Dart direct | Higher volume | Contract rates |
| India Post (Speed Post) | Remote PIN codes | Cheap, wide reach, slower |

- Delivery estimate from Azamgarh: typically ~3–8 days depending on PIN code. Confirm with courier.
- **PIN code check** on product pages (6 digits): show serviceability and an estimated date once the courier API is connected.
- **Fragile items** (black pottery): double box, honeycomb paper / moulded inserts, "Fragile" label; consider shipping insurance. Do drop tests.
- Volumetric weight matters: keep box sizes tight.
- **COD**: allow with a limit and a small fee; verify phone by WhatsApp before dispatch to cut RTO (return to origin).

## Tax: GST
- Selling goods online across states generally needs **GST registration**. There are relaxations for small sellers in some cases. **Confirm with a CA.**
- Each product needs an **HSN code** and GST rate (handicrafts, textiles and pottery have different rates).
- Prices shown **inclusive of GST**; issue GST invoices.

## Legal & compliance (e-commerce)
- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**: show legal name, address, contact details and a **grievance officer**; clear return/refund/exchange, shipping, payment and cancellation policies; total price including charges; country of origin.
- **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)**: MRP, net quantity, manufacturer name/address and consumer-care details on packaged goods.
- **DPDP Act 2023** (data protection): privacy policy, consent for marketing messages.
- Food items (sweets) need an **FSSAI** licence. Avoid them until licensed.
- **GI tag**: "Nizamabad Black Pottery" is a registered GI. Only use the GI name/logo if the maker is an authorised user.

## Business setup checklist
- [ ] Business entity (proprietorship to start, LLP later)
- [ ] PAN, current bank account
- [ ] GST registration (+ HSN codes)
- [ ] Udyam (MSME) registration (free, useful for schemes)
- [ ] Payment gateway KYC (Razorpay)
- [ ] Shiprocket account + pickup address in Azamgarh
- [ ] Optional: Artisan card / handicrafts registration, GI authorised-user registration
