# 06 · Shipping, Customs & Legal

> ⚠️ Not legal or tax advice. Rules change. **Verify each item with a CA (India) and a customs broker / accountant (GCC) before launch.**

## Fulfilment models (decision D-001)
| Model | Delivery time | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Ship each order from India (courier) | ~4–10 days | No GCC company or stock needed, low risk | Slower, per-parcel cost high, customs duty for the recipient, no "same-day" gifting |
| B. GCC hub (stock in Kuwait / UAE, local delivery) | Same / next day | Bleems-like experience, COD possible | Needs a local partner or company, bulk import, warehouse |
| C. Hybrid (**recommended**) | Hub items fast, others 4–10 days | Best of both | More complex ops. Show delivery speed per product |

## Couriers to evaluate
- **India → GCC:** DHL Express, Aramex, FedEx, Shiprocket X, DTDC International, India Post (cheapest, slowest)
- **Local GCC last mile:** Aramex, SMSA (KSA), local Kuwaiti delivery companies, Quiqup / Careem-style on-demand (UAE)

## Customs & duties
- GCC common customs tariff: **~5%** on most goods, plus local **VAT** where applicable (see `03`). Verify de-minimis thresholds per country.
- Decide **DDP** (we pay duties, customer sees the final price, which is best for gifts) vs **DDU** (recipient pays, which is bad for surprises).
  **Recommendation: DDP** for gifts.
- Every product needs an **HS code**, value and material description on the commercial invoice.
- Restricted or sensitive items: food (sweets), perfumes / attar (alcohol-based attars restricted; also dangerous-goods rules for air freight), and anything with religious text needs careful labelling. Check each one.

## India-side registration (typical for exporters)
- [ ] Business entity (proprietorship / LLP / Pvt Ltd)
- [ ] **GST registration** + **LUT** (Letter of Undertaking) for zero-rated exports
- [ ] **IEC** (Import Export Code) from DGFT
- [ ] Current bank account with foreign remittance capability (FIRC / e-BRC)
- [ ] Optional: Handicrafts EPCH membership, artisan card, GI authorised-user registration (for Nizamabad pottery claims)

## GCC-side (only if hub model B / C)
- Local commercial registration, or partner with an existing licensed company
- E-commerce licence where required (e.g. Kuwait MOCI, UAE e-trader / DED, KSA Maroof / e-commerce law)
- VAT registration if over the threshold (KSA, UAE, Bahrain, Oman)

## Website policies (must exist at launch, EN + AR)
- Shipping policy (times per country, DDP note, tracking)
- Returns & refunds (handmade / personalised items are often non-returnable; damaged-on-arrival process with photo)
- Privacy policy (KSA PDPL, UAE PDPL, India DPDP Act)
- Terms & conditions
- Cookie notice

## Packaging (fragile craft!)
- Double-box black pottery, with molded inserts or honeycomb paper; run drop tests before launch
- Branded premium gift box + tissue + thank-you card (EN / AR)
- No price on the packing slip for gifts
